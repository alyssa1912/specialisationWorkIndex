Project Synopsis

This project is an interactive musical instrument designed for toddlers, allowing them to create sounds by pressing letter keys on a computer keyboard. Each key produces a different sound and displays a different star animation, allowing children to build their own colourful night sky as they explore music through play. The instrument combines sound, movement, and simple visual feedback to create an engaging and intuitive experience without relying on written instructions or musical knowledge. The project encourages young children to experiment with sounds, discover cause and effect, and enjoy creative musical exploration.

Design Scenario and Response

Chosen Scenario: Musical Toy for Toddlers

Prompt - Design a way for young children with minimal language skills to explore music.

The design scenario explores how many browser-based instruments are powerful tools for creating music and are often designed for adults or experienced users who mostly rely on written instructions or their understanding of musical concepts to create compositions. While this offers a high level of creative freedom, they are not accessible, engaging or comprehensible for younger users such as toddlers, as many of these interfaces require users to interpret text-based instructions, navigate menus and understand musical concepts before they can interact with the instrument. For toddlers whose language and motor skills are still developing, this can be a barrier that discourages them from using these tools.

My project responds to the scenario by creating a simple, interactive musical instrument that allows toddlers to explore sound through experimentation. By pressing different keys on a keyboard, children can trigger unique sounds paired with exciting visual animations. This creates a direct connection between their actions and the audio-visual feedback they receive. The instrument uses playful animations, visually aesthetic themes and simple interactions to make the experience engaging and approachable for young children. As toddlers have limited language skills, the design reduces reliance on text and communicates through visual cues, movement and sound. The experience encourages curiosity and independent exploration without requiring musical knowledge or complicated controls.
